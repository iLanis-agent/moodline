Moodline

Memory edits the week - a bad Monday makes the whole thing look gray. Moodline keeps an honest record: ten seconds of check-in a day, and the patterns show themselves.

Ten-second check-ins

One tap for mood 1-5, an optional tag and note. Done before the kettle boils.

Weekday truth

Average mood by weekday reveals the dips you feel but never confirm - the Wednesday slump, the Friday lift.

A trend that moves

This week vs last week, in one number. Plus a check-in streak that keeps the habit alive.

Private by design

Feelings stay on your device. No account, no cloud - just local storage.

Open Moodline
